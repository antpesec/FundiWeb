Explore our FAQs

Answers about process, pricing, timelines, and what working together looks like.

How do we start a project?

Send a short note about the business, what you need, and when you would like it live. We reply within one business day with a few questions or a time to talk.

How long does a website take?

A focused website is usually two to three weeks after we agree the scope and have the words and photos we need. Apps take longer because the flows need more care.

Do you build apps as well as websites?

Yes. Websites for customers who are finding you, and web apps for bookings, accounts, dashboards, and day-to-day operations.

Can you work with a site we already have?

Often, yes. We can redesign what is there, or leave the public site and build the app beside it. We will say honestly if a fresh start is the cleaner path.

Do we need to write everything ourselves?

You know the business, so we start from your notes. We help shape the structure and the wording so the pages are clear, without turning the project into a long writing assignment.

What happens after launch?

You get a walkthrough and the access you need. Ongoing care is available for edits and small improvements, so the work does not stall the week after it goes live.

How do payments work?

A typical project is split in two: half to begin, and half before launch. Custom scopes are written out before any work starts.

Do you work with businesses outside one city?

Yes. Projects are run remotely, with calls and shared reviews. Location does not change the process.
